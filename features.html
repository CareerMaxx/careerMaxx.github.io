Our Features.

Find opportunities. Gain experience. Become qualified.

Discover how you can easly find new opportunities to gain experience and pave your career path.

01

Smart search

Find free programs, clubs and even internships and co-op opportunities.

Co-ops and Internships

Smart Search easily picks out the best positions tuned to your interests and experience level. Use the advanced filter feature to further personalize your results.

Courses, Training and Workshops

Find experiences that match your level and interests to build solid technical and trasnferable skills for you resume.

Clubs and Volunteer Opportunities

Its important to get involved in the community. Having joining clubs or volunteering can significantly boost your resume and jump start you career!

02

AI Resume Scorer

Analyse your resume, discover your gaps and Maxximize your potential.

Resume Scorer

An advanced algorithm scans your resume and gives it a score our of 100 points based on structure, formatting, consistency and content relevance.

Skill Analysis

Analyses your skills, studies your level of expertise and finds gaps in your examples.

AI Feedback and Tailoring

Suggests improvements tailoired to your prefered future position. Helps you fix the structure of your resume and personolizes it to include key-words.


03

Career Path

Discover what you need to lean to adapt and achieve your career goals.

Career Goal Selection

Find out what you really want to be in the future based on your curent interests and experience, and compare between different career opportunities.

Career Roadmap

Gives you a clear route from where you currently are to your future role. Visualizes your experiences and maps out a sequence of tailored steps based on your interests and background.

Skill Gap Improvements

Compares your current skill and shows exactly what's missing. Each gap is paired with concrete actions that link back to Smart Search, where you'll find what you need to advance.
